How ArtPalette works

Three simple steps turn two images into one new artistic interpretation.

1

Choose a photograph

Upload the image you want to transform. It provides the structure, objects and composition of the final artwork.

2

Choose a style

Upload an artwork or visual reference. AdaIN extracts its visual statistics and applies them to the content representation.

3

Create your artwork

Adjust the style strength and let the trained decoder reconstruct the stylized image for you.

About the project

Where technology meets visual expression.

ArtPalette is an AI-powered neural style transfer application built around Adaptive Instance Normalization (AdaIN). It combines a content image with a style reference and uses a VGG-based encoder and trained decoder to generate a new stylized image.

The application is served through Flask and provides an interactive interface for uploading images, controlling style strength and downloading the generated artwork.

Frequently asked questions

Got Questions?

ArtPalette uses Adaptive Instance Normalization (AdaIN) to transfer the visual characteristics of a style reference to a content image.
JPG, JPEG and PNG images are supported.
Processing time depends on the image size and the hardware running the application.
Images are handled by the local Flask application and stored in its configured uploads folder.
